# Design: First paying ateliers, then a promo + capped Meta test

## Problem Statement

The founder wants Claude Code to run a promo pipeline end to end: a fake demo atelier
("أتيليه لمسة", Nasr City), mobile and desktop RTL screenshots, a 27-second promo video,
and Meta ads aimed at atelier owners in Nasr City. The plan came out of a chat about
"making money online easily".

The session found that the real problem is different. Gateling Atelier has warm demand
but no ask in the funnel. The ad plan stays, but it sits behind a conversion sprint.

## Demand Evidence

- **One daily user, no revenue.** Atelier Alaa El-Kasry runs on the system (about 1,100
  of its own customers in the data) and does not pay.
- **Pipeline (gateling-sales, 2026-11-02).** 343 leads. The founder: "240 of them was
  auto parked due to i just wanted to keep top 100 active". Of the active 100: "50 reply
  and give me real business pain", "the other 50 will just say not interested or not
  respond at all".
- **No conversion step yet.** 0 leads at demo_agreed or later. The founder has not asked
  any of them for a demo or a price yet.
- **Reading.** A 50% reply rate with stated pain is strong interest. It is not demand yet:
  nobody has been offered a price, so nobody has had the chance to say yes.

## Status Quo

A paper notebook or diary at the shop counter. It is free, always there, and needs no
training. It fails on double-bookings of the same dress, on deposits nobody can
reconcile, and whenever the person who keeps it is away. The pitch has to name the
notebook's failure moment, not list features.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

- **User.** The owner of a dress-rental atelier in Cairo who keeps bookings in a
  notebook. The first ten are named: the strongest of the ~50 pain replies in
  gateling-sales.
- **Wedge.** "We set your atelier up for you, branded with your name and dresses, today."
  The founder enters the notebook data. A price is stated in the first message, with the
  first month free. The per-prospect `/atelier-demo` already delivers the branded part.

## Constraints

- **Solo founder.** Every hour spent on ads or video is an hour not spent on asks.
- **Data safety.**
  `.env` is production Neon, and `.env.local` is a local DB that holds real customer
  data. The marketing seed must run against a third, dedicated local database and refuse
  any other target.
- **Messaging.** The gateling-sales MCP never sends messages. Claude drafts, the founder
  sends, and the founder logs outcomes. Never send a third unanswered WhatsApp message.
- **Spending.** Every ad step that spends money gets explicit founder confirmation.
- **Public assets.** Fake data only: fake Egyptian names and phones on an unassigned
  prefix (see phase 2). Dress photos come from Pexels or Unsplash, with a credits file.
  No faces as the focus. Real people (Alaa) appear only with written consent.

## Premises

1. **Conversion is the bottleneck, not reach.** The ~50 warm leads come first.
2. **Alaa El-Kasry is the first proof.** Ask them to pay, and for a 30-second Arabic
   phone clip at the counter.
3. **The screenshots and video are first a WhatsApp sales asset**, and second the ad
   creative.
4. **(Revised by founder, then sequenced)** A small click-to-WhatsApp Meta test runs in
   parallel, but ad money moves only after the first 10 priced asks are sent.
5. **This is conversation-led sales, not passive income.** Claude does the production
   work; the founder holds the conversations.

## Cross-Model Perspective

Cold read by a fresh Claude subagent:

- **Steelman.** It replaces the paper notebook at Cairo dress-rental counters. Its edge
  is a demo branded with the prospect's own name, logo and dresses in minutes, which no
  generic competitor can match.
- **Key insight.** "The funnel is not leaking; there is simply no ask in it." The video
  and ad pipeline risk becoming a way to avoid asking for money.
- **Challenged #4.** It said a cold ad can't be read while the warm conversion rate is
  unknown. Falsifier: send 20 warm leads a demo-plus-price ask; if fewer than 3 book,
  cold ads will do worse.
- **Its 48-hour plan.** Get Alaa's clip, run `/atelier-demo` for the 10 strongest
  replies, then send each one their link, a voice note, the clip, a price, and a
  15-minute call slot.
- **Outcome.** The founder kept the ad test but accepted the sequencing: ads go live
  after the 10 asks.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Warm-ask sprint only
No new code: `/atelier-demo`, the Alaa clip, and priced asks. It is the fastest route to a
real conversion rate. **Not chosen as the whole plan**, because the founder wants the
promo assets and an ad test. It becomes phase 1 of B.

### Approach B: Asks first, then asset pipeline + capped click-to-WhatsApp test (CHOSEN)
See Recommended Approach.

### Approach C: Personal demo as the ad
An ad offer of "your atelier, live in 10 minutes", where every ad lead gets an
`/atelier-demo`. **Not chosen now.** Keep it as the next ad variant if the generic promo
underperforms, since it is the most differentiated offer.

## Recommended Approach

Approach B, in three phases. Phase 2 runs in parallel with phase 1; phase 3 is gated on
phase 1's outcome.

### Phase 1: Conversion sprint (days 1–2, founder-led, Claude assists)

1. **Founder sets a monthly price**, with "first month free, we set it up for you". This
   blocks everything else.
2. **Ask Alaa El-Kasry** for three things:
   - a payment, even a small one
   - a 30-second Arabic phone clip at the counter ("before, the notebook; now, this")
   - **separate written consent (on WhatsApp) for each use**: (a) sending the clip to
     prospects, and (b) using it in paid Meta ads. Record both answers in gateling-sales.

   **Clip review rule.** No customer faces, no readable notebook entries, names or
   phones. Crop or blur, or drop the clip. Without ad consent, the ad cut uses the
   version without the clip.
3. **Claude shortlists the 10 strongest pain replies** from gateling-sales and runs
   `/atelier-demo` for each (existing flow, review gate unchanged).
   - **Capacity check first:** Neon project quota and Vercel domain limits for 10
     concurrent demos. If the concurrent limit is N < 10, demo only the top N leads; the rest get the priced ask with Alaa's clip and a link to one shared fake demo. Upgrading the Neon plan instead needs founder approval of the cost.
   - Budget about 15 minutes of founder review per demo.
   - Demos keep the skill's 7-day lifetime. A demo with no booked call at day 7 is torn
     down with `/atelier-demo teardown <slug>`.
4. **Claude drafts one Arabic WhatsApp message per lead**: their own pain in their words,
   their branded link, the price, and a 15-minute call slot. The founder sends them and
   logs each ask, objection and booked demo with `log_outcome`.

### Phase 2: Promo asset pipeline (Claude builds, parallel to phase 1)

**Isolation is the design. The real-customer DB is never on the path.**

1. **Separate Postgres service.** Add a second compose service, `postgres-marketing`, on
   port 5433 with its own volume `pgdata-marketing` and `POSTGRES_DB=atelier_marketing`.
   It never shares a server or volume with the `.env.local` database. Migrations run
   with `drizzle-kit migrate` and an explicit `DATABASE_URL` for that service.
2. **Credentials and env.** `.env.marketing.local` (gitignored) holds three values:
   - `DATABASE_URL`: the 5433 / `atelier_marketing` URL
   - `MARKETING_ADMIN_EMAIL`
   - `MARKETING_ADMIN_PASSWORD`, generated once

   The seed and the screenshot script both read only this file. The password is shown
   to the founder once in chat. Re-seeding keeps it unless `MARKETING_ROTATE_PASSWORD=1`.
3. **New seed entry point** (`src/drizzle/seed/marketing/`, `npm run marketing:seed`,
   `npm run marketing:reset`).
   - **Guard.** It has its own guard and does **not** call `assertClientDemoTarget`. It
     refuses unless the URL host is `localhost`/`127.0.0.1`/`::1`, the port is 5433 and
     the database is `atelier_marketing`. It takes `DATABASE_URL` only from
     `.env.marketing.local`, never `.env.local`.
   - **Reset.** `marketing:reset` truncates tables only after the same guard passes.
     Before the first write it prints the resolved target and waits for confirmation.
   - **Data.** It generates its own data:
     - the atelier "أتيليه لمسة" in مدينة نصر
     - 12–15 dresses with Arabic names and colours, real sizes, 1,500–8,000 EGP and
       existing-format reference codes
     - 20–30 reservations over the next 30 days, mixing confirmed, picked-up and
       returned, with deposits
     - expense history
   - **Money invariant.** Payments go through the same helpers the demo seed uses, so
     the money invariant in `docs/seeding-and-demo-data.md` holds. `scripts/smoke-dashboard.ts`
     runs against it after seeding.
4. **Fake people that can't reach anyone.**
   - Fake Egyptian names.
   - Phones use the **unassigned 016 prefix** (for example `0160 000 0123`). 010, 011,
     012 and 015 are live operators.
   - If the app's phone validation rejects 016, phones are seeded valid but every phone
     cell is blurred in the screenshots, and the reviewer checks it.
5. **Images stay local.** Dress photos (Pexels/Unsplash, mannequins or hangers) are
   downloaded into `public/marketing/dresses/` (gitignored). The seed stores those
   relative URLs, so nothing goes to the production Firebase bucket.
   - Every source URL and photographer goes in `marketing/credits.md`.
   - If the app's image component only accepts Firebase URLs, the fallback is the
     Firebase Storage emulator, not the real bucket.
6. **Served app is provably the marketing dataset.** `npm run marketing:dev` starts
   `next dev -p 3100` with `DATABASE_URL` exported from `.env.marketing.local` into the
   process environment. Next.js does not override an existing process variable with
   `.env.local`.

   The screenshot script (`scripts/marketing/screenshots.ts`, Playwright) runs a
   pre-capture guard. It logs in, opens the dashboard, and aborts unless the atelier name
   is "أتيليه لمسة" and no customer outside the seeded fake-name list appears on the
   first page.
7. **Screenshots.** The script captures Arabic/RTL in two sizes, mobile 430×932 @3x
   (minimum set) and desktop 1440×900 @2x. It hides dev overlays, waits for
   `networkidle` and every `img.complete`, and writes
   `marketing/screenshots/NN-name-{mobile,desktop}.png`. Screens:
   1. dress list
   2. one dress's availability calendar
   3. a new reservation with a deposit
   4. upcoming fittings: the existing reservations view filtered to upcoming (see Open
      Questions)
   5. dashboard

   **The founder eyeballs every PNG before it leaves the machine.**
8. **Video.** 27 seconds in 9:16 (minimum), built in Moda from the mobile screenshots,
   with Arabic captions.
   - 0–4s: the notebook pain, using Alaa's clip only with ad consent
   - 4–22s: the screens
   - 22–27s: the WhatsApp CTA

   The first use is phase 1 follow-ups.
9. **Gate.** No app UI change is expected. If screen 4 needs a new filtered view, that
   is a UI change: `/ui-scan --plan` first, `/ui-scan` after. Then `typecheck && build`.

### Phase 3: Capped Meta click-to-WhatsApp test (gated)

- **Spend gate (all three must hold):**
  1. 10 priced asks are logged.
  2. **At least 2 demos booked** from those 10, or the 2026-11-09 checkpoint is reached.
     At the checkpoint, if fewer than 2 are booked, ads launch only on an explicit
     founder override, recorded in this doc with the reason. That falls short of the
     cross-model falsifier's bar (3 of 20), and the founder accepts that ad results may
     be worse than warm results.
  3. The founder approves the creative and budget.
- **Setup.** Through the Adspirer `meta_ads` tools: one click-to-WhatsApp campaign.
  - Lifetime budget **exactly 1,500 EGP** over 7 days.
  - Placement: Nasr City, 5 km radius, Arabic, ages 25–55.
  - Meta cannot target by occupation, so there is no interest targeting. The creative's
    first line ("لو عندك أتيليه فساتين…") does the filtering.

  Every spending call is confirmed by the founder first.
- **Intake and attribution.** Each click-to-WhatsApp conversation becomes a lead via
  `create_lead`, with the note `source: meta_ctwa, campaign <id>`. The founder reports
  it, and Claude logs it. Booked demos go through `log_outcome`. On day 7, Claude
  computes cost per conversation and cost per booked demo from gateling-sales plus
  Adspirer spend.
- **Decision on day 7:**
  - **Keep** if at least 2 ad-sourced demos are booked (cost per demo of 750 EGP or
    less). Next round: 3,000 EGP over 14 days, same targeting, with fresh founder
    approval.
  - **Iterate** if exactly 1 demo is booked. One more 7-day round at 1,500 EGP with
    Approach C's "your atelier in 10 minutes" creative, then decide with the same rule.
  - **Stop** if 0 demos are booked. No more ad spend until warm conversion improves.

## Open Questions

1. **What is the monthly price?** This blocks phase 1. The founder decides.
2. **Fittings screen.** Is "upcoming fittings" a reservation attribute that a filtered
   reservations view can show? Or does screen 4 become "upcoming reservations"? Decide
   before the screenshot script. A new view triggers the UI gate.
3. **Validation.** Does the app's phone validation accept the 016 prefix, and does its
   image component accept relative `/marketing/...` URLs? Check in code before seeding.
   The fallbacks are defined above.
4. **Meta access.** Is the Meta ad account connected in Adspirer, and is the WhatsApp
   Business number linked for click-to-WhatsApp?

## Success Criteria

- **By 2026-11-04:** price set, 10 priced asks sent and logged, Alaa asked (pay, clip,
  two consents).
- **By 2026-11-09:** at least 2 demos booked from the 10 warm asks, and the phase 2
  minimum assets (mobile screenshots + 9:16 video) are reviewed and in `marketing/`.
- **By 2026-11-16:** at least 1 paying atelier (Alaa counts).
- **Ad test:** on day 7, keep, iterate or stop under the rule above, reported as cost
  per conversation and cost per booked demo.

## Reviewer Concerns

### R2-1 — feasibility (blocking)

**Problem**

> The capacity fallback does not address the constraint it names. The /atelier-demo skill creates one Neon project per client (atelier-demo-<slug>) and gives each demo a 7-day lifetime, and phase 1 keeps that lifetime. Demos created on day 1 are therefore still alive on day 2, so splitting creation 5/5 over two days still needs 10 concurrent Neon projects and 10 branch domains. If the plan allows fewer than 10, the second batch hits the plan-limit error, and the skill then says to stop without deleting anything. Phase 1, which gates phase 3, would stall partway through.

**Remedy**

> Make the fallback match a concurrent limit. For example: if the quota is N < 10, demo only the top N leads and send the rest the price ask with Alaa's clip or a shared demo link; or upgrade the Neon plan first, with founder approval of the cost; or shorten the lifetime and tear down non-responders before creating the next batch. State which one applies and how many demos can be live at once.

### R2-2 — consistency (minor)

**Problem**

> Premise 4 ('ad money moves only after the first 10 priced asks are sent') and the Cross-Model Perspective outcome ('ads go live after the 10 asks') still describe the old gate. The phase 3 spend gate now also requires at least 2 booked demos, or a recorded founder override at the 2026-11-09 checkpoint.

**Remedy**

> Update premise 4 and the cross-model outcome line to point to the three-part spend gate.

### R2-3 — clarity (minor)

**Problem**

> The Iterate branch re-applies the same rule, so repeated 1-demo results can produce an unbounded run of 1,500 EGP rounds. The doc also does not say whether the Iterate round needs fresh founder approval of the new creative, the way Keep does.

**Remedy**

> Cap iteration, for example at one Iterate round, after which 1 demo counts as Stop. Say explicitly that the Iterate round needs fresh founder approval of the creative and budget.

### R2-4 — completeness (minor)

**Problem**

**Remedy**

> Once the price is set, confirm 750 EGP per demo is affordable given the expected demo-to-paid rate. Or add a condition that at least one ad-sourced demo has reached a paid or committed step before the 3,000 EGP round.

**Remedy**

**Remedy**

**Remedy**
